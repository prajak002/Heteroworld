What is real here

Buildings, sidewalks, tree pits and street-tree positions come from NYC Open Data at this block. The six robots and four pedestrians on the sidewalk replay one recorded simulator episode (seed 20250019). Every other person and car is ambient life added for context and is not part of the experiment. Bodies are NVIDIA SOMA-X, robots are the Unitree G1 model; both walk with procedural gaits.

Final campaign · 3 run seeds per setting · paired McNemar tests, Holm-corrected

What the full campaign shows

Every planner was run on identical scenarios, so each comparison is paired episode by episode. Dots are pooled rates with 95% Wilson intervals; hover a dot for the paired test against HETERO-WORLD.

All methods, all settings (table)

Synthetic settings use the hand-built heterogeneous world models; the two "learned" settings use the trained diffusion, JEPA and RSSM models for pedestrian forecasts. NYC sidewalk types are the four k-means clusters of 544 measured block faces (NYC Open Data). NYC shards ran partly on an Apple M1 and partly on x86; a re-run of one shard on x86 matched every episode outcome (25/25) with small trajectory differences in 4 episodes.

Scene data: NYC Open Data building footprints (5zhs-2jue), planimetric sidewalks (52n9-sdep), 2015 Street Tree Census (uvpi-gqnh). Bodies: NVIDIA SOMA-X (Apache-2.0).
